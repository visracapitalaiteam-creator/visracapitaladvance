Privacy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Visra Capital Group, based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For any question about this notice, write to visracapitalMD@gmail.com.

What the website itself collects

Very little. This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics, and it does not ask you to create an account.

The enquiry form does not send anything to us through the website. When you press send, it opens your own email application with your message already written and addressed to us. Nothing reaches us unless you then choose to send that email.

What you send us

If you email us, we receive what you include: typically your name, email address, company or family office, area of interest, and your message. We use it to respond to you and to continue the conversation you started.

We treat every enquiry in strict confidence, and we do not sell or share your details.

Services that help deliver this website

  • Hosting. Like any website, the service that hosts these pages receives standard technical information when your browser requests them, such as your IP address.
  • Typefaces. The fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google, which receives your IP address.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time what information we hold from your enquiry, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to visracapitalMD@gmail.com.

Changes to this notice

If we change how this website handles information, we will update this page and the date above.
